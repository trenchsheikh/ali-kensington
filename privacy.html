We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Notice explains how we collect and use your information when you contact us or instruct us in relation to immigration legal services.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, contact details, identification and passport information, immigration history, family and employment information, financial information, and other information relevant to your immigration matter. This may include sensitive personal information where necessary.

How we use your information

We use your information to provide legal advice and immigration services, prepare and submit applications and appeals, communicate with relevant authorities, manage your matter, comply with our legal and regulatory obligations, and communicate with you about our services.

Confidentiality and sharing

We keep your information confidential in accordance with our professional obligations as an SRA-regulated law firm. Where necessary and lawful, we may share relevant information with the Home Office, UK Visas and Immigration, courts and tribunals, barristers, experts, interpreters, professional advisers and other relevant organisations.

Data security and retention

We take appropriate measures to keep your information secure and retain it only for as long as reasonably necessary, taking account of our legal, regulatory and professional obligations.

Your rights

Under applicable data protection law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. These rights are subject to legal exceptions.
