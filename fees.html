What is included

Our professional fees will generally include:

  • taking your instructions and providing legal advice;
  • assessing your eligibility;
  • reviewing supporting documents;
  • preparing the application or appeal;
  • completing the relevant forms;
  • preparing supporting representations where appropriate;
  • submitting the application or appeal; and
  • advising you about the process and outcome.

The precise scope of work will be confirmed in your Client Care Letter.

Additional costs

Our professional fees do not normally include third-party costs such as:

  • Home Office application fees;
  • Immigration Health Surcharge;
  • priority or super-priority fees;
  • biometric or appointment fees;
  • translation and interpreting costs;
  • expert reports; or
  • barrister or counsel fees.

We will advise you of applicable additional costs before they are incurred.

VAT

VAT may be applicable to our professional fees depending on your circumstances. Where VAT is payable, it is charged at the applicable rate of 20%. We will confirm the VAT position when you instruct us.

Timescales

For a straightforward matter, preparation will generally take approximately 2–6 weeks from receiving the necessary information and documents.

Home Office and tribunal processing times are outside our control and vary according to the type of matter.

Who will handle your matter?

Your matter will be handled by an appropriately qualified member of our immigration team and supervised in accordance with our professional obligations. The relevant fee earner and supervisor, including their qualifications and experience, will be identified when you instruct us.

Important information

These are indicative fee ranges, not fixed quotations. The final fee will depend on the circumstances and complexity of your case.

Asylum applications: The SRA's mandatory price-transparency requirements do not apply to asylum applications. Fees for asylum work will be discussed separately.
